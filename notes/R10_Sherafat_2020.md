**In one sentence:** 
State of the art review in automated methods for activity recognition of construction workers and equipment, focus on audio-based methods, kinematic-based methods and vision-based method

## 1. Problem
<!-- What question does the paper try to answer? What gap did the authors see? -->
The authors analyzed the feasiability to implement automated algorithm for monitoring workers and equipments on construction sites.  

## 2. Method
<!-- How did they answer it? Model, pipeline, key idea. Plain words, not their abstract. -->
Review of 130 articles grouped in 3 categories : (1) kinematic-based, (2) vision-based, and (3) audio-based. The authors describe the most relevant methods developed regarding activity detection and activity recognition. After that, they discussed advantages and diadvantages, evaluation of effectiveness, and limitations. 

## 3. Data
<!-- Which dataset? Images or video? Real or synthetic? How many? Which machines / activities? Public? -->
The articles mentioned in the state of the art review were trained with real data. Synthetic data was not mentioned at all in the review. Through the study is difficult estimate the quantity and quality of the data used in each study. To dive on it, it is necessary to check every cited article. 

## 4. Result
<!-- The main numbers (with the metric name) and what they mean. -->
The key results were qualitative after an evaluation of 130 articles. The most striking conclusion is that developing hybrid methods using fusion techniques to combine results for improving the overall performance would be extremely useful. Boadly speaking, every technique has its limitations due to the jobsite condition factors, let say : weather, jobsite size, congestion of jobsite, multiple equipement or workers, need of several devices, etc.  

## 5. Limitation
<!-- What the paper does NOT solve: stated by the authors, or what you noticed yourself. -->
From my perspective, the evaluation must be executed using a universal dataset in every group of techniques. Inside that frame, the analysis and evaluation is reaonable.
