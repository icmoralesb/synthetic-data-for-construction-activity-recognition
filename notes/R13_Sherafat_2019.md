**In one sentence:** 
Fusion of two categories of automated ativity recongnition of construction equipment: kinematic-based method and audio-based method.

## 1. Data
<!-- Which dataset? Images or video? Real or synthetic? How many? Which machines / activities? Public? -->
Real data was collected direct in jobsite using an iPhone to measure acceleration and angular velocity and microphone to record the noise.  

## 2. Link to my work
<!-- How does this help, support or challenge MY project? Which research question (RQ1–RQ4)? -->
This study show fusion of two methods : kinematic-based method and audio-based method. According to authors it worked with some interference given the jobsite conditions. Additional, the data was collected for only one type of equipment, it means a very poor variability of the data. Synthetic data can cover this lack of data needed for train a model properly.
