## 1. Data

Real data. Training data was collected using sensor data, 5 construction workers, 11 body parts monitored and 6 different construction-realted activities. One limitation mentioned in the articles was the relatively small sample size: limited participants. Therefore movement patterns, task execution style and body types were not fully captured. Data were collected under controlled conditions: weather changes, apatial constraints or team-based interactions were not assessed.
